Mentally Strong

Mentally strong

info

A mental health and personal growth website combining psychiatric care, coaching programs, educational resources, and the Mentally STRONG Method. I worked on building and organizing the website’s pages and layouts, focusing on clear content structure, responsive design, and an experience that feels professional while still being approachable.

Year

2025

Project

  • MENTALLY STRONG

Contribution

I was responsible for building and customizing the website’s pages using Elementor. This included creating page layouts, structuring content, building sections, styling elements, and making sure the pages worked properly across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

I also worked on organizing the site’s different sections and service pages so visitors could easily move between private programs, clinic services, specialty treatments, educational content, team information, and contact or appointment pages.

A major part of the work was keeping the website visually consistent across a large number of pages while still giving each section enough space to clearly present its information.
